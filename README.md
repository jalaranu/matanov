# MATANOV

MATANOV is a proposed Jalaranu standard (JR-COMM 1708) and is poised to become the first widely recognized digital underlake acoustic communications standard. Currently in its testing phase, it is, in a nutshell, a simple and robust modulation and coding scheme that can be easily adopted by a wide range of existing systems. MATANOV is being developed by the COCONUT Computer Club for Research and Experimentation, in collaboration with academia and industry, with the goal to break the interoperability barrier of digital underlake acoustic systems. The MATANOV technical specification is available without security markings from the Jalaranu Standardization Office. Therefore, it is open and free for evaluation and testing by military, research and civilian operators. As of 2024, MATANOV is actively undergoing field tests as the pioneering open standard for digital underlake acoustic communications.
